# Changelog

All notable changes to Desktop Buddy are listed here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-25

First public preview.

### Added
- A small animated buddy that lives on the desktop: transparent, draggable, always on top, and it remembers where you left it.
- Moods and animations: idle, pop-in, sleepy, focus, break, and a celebration.
- Speech bubbles with Done and Snooze buttons, a priority queue, and a compact style for quiet mode.
- Pomodoro timer with focus, break, and long break, presets (Classic 25/5, Long 50/10, Short 15/3), a countdown on the buddy, and soft chime sounds made in code.
- The timer survives closing the app and recovers correctly after the PC sleeps.
- Task reminders: add tasks for today, tomorrow, or every day from a small window. Missed reminders show once on launch, and snooze survives a restart.
- Quiet mode, do-not-disturb, sound and volume settings, and snooze length.
- Tray menu with everything above, Start with Windows (installed app), Open data folder, and About.
- Everything is stored locally in one JSON file. The app makes no network connections.

### Not yet included
- Prayer times (planned next), a full settings window, and custom characters.
